## 2020

<time datetime="2020-11-01"></time> until <time datetime="2021-03-01"></time>, Londrina, Paraná, Brazil

## 2021

### Engineering Manager · Bis2Bis E-commerce

<time datetime="2021-03-01"></time> until <time datetime="2022-03-01"></time>, Londrina, Paraná, Brazil

Here the challenge was more about people than code. About 40 people were splitting their time between planned work, incidents, and customer requests, all at once.

I organized the team into five streams (support, implementation, integrations, maintenance, and urgent requests), each with an owner, an agreed deadline, and a clear way to ask for help.

The planned work that actually shipped went from about 60% to more than 80%. And with Docker and GitLab, we cut about 80% of the manual steps when releasing.

## 2022

### Software Architect · Bis2Bis E-commerce

<time datetime="2022-03-01"></time> until <time datetime="2024-04-01"></time>, Londrina, Paraná, Brazil

At Bis2Bis, I moved into an architect role. The platform already had about 1,200 live projects and about 40 developers, and what was missing was a common direction instead of a different decision for every application.

I drew the architecture path and led the move of part of the operation from AWS to Huawei Cloud, always making clear what we gained and what we paid in scale, cost, and availability.

With shared standards and design reviews, release-related failures dropped by about 40%, and unavailability by about 15%.

And I left a simple rule for choosing technology: the one that fits the work and the team's ability to keep it running.
