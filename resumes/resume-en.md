### Proof of Erasure

Since <time datetime="2025-08-01"></time>.

Research on the tension between blockchain immutability and the data-erasure rights of the LGPD and GDPR. It proposes a distributed architecture that combines encrypted data, versioned updates, and forensic fingerprinting with a three-layer Proof of Erasure. The system gives verifiable assurance that deleted information cannot be reconstructed, even on open networks. The implementation uses the Solana development environment with off-chain cryptography, and shows that a blockchain can both keep immutable records and provably "forget."
